A first description

Here are the main features of the site, implemented according to this style:

  • A top bar containing the site title and links to other pages.
  • A simple footer.
  • A patterned background inspired by WhatsApp and Telegram, the platforms through which we most frequently interact with a computer.
  • The article itself, consisting of:
    1. A header with a title and description.
    2. A main section for developing your content.

When making an article...

Many graphical elements are ready to be used, but make sure to do the following first:

  • Copy example-article.html. You can find it here.
  • Follow the commented instructions inside it:
    • Update the <body> class with the correct topic to keep colours consistent.
    • Update the "previous" and "next" links in the body's <nav>.
    • Update the title, description, and info pills in the body's header.
    • Write your article inside <main>, keeping accessibility best

Colours

Every coloured element can take one of three kinds of colour. Add the class next to the element's own class:

  • Default: no extra class, the site blue.
  • Topic: topic-..., the colour of a topic. Put it on <body> to colour the whole page, or on a single card.
  • Status: good, warn or bad, for a single element that says if something is right or wrong.

Comparison table

All tables automatically get styled headings, alternating row colours and highlighted rows on hover. Put a wide table inside a box table-scroll to scroll wide content sideways on small screens, using touch or the arrow keys when the box has keyboard focus.

Comparing input methods
Input method Common use Strength Design consideration
Keyboard Writing and navigating forms Precise input and shortcuts Keep focus visible and follow a logical tab order.
Mouse Selecting and moving objects Direct pointing on a large screen Provide keyboard alternatives to pointing and dragging.
Touchscreen Using phones and tablets Interacting directly with content Make targets large enough and leave space between them.
Voice Hands-free commands and dictation Input without touching a device Offer another input method for noisy or shared spaces.

Topic navigation

The homepage has a Topics dropdown with two levels: topics and subtopic links. Click a topic row to open its subtopics. Native <details> and <summary> work with a mouse, touch or keyboard, without JavaScript.

Try opening Topics, then Input/output devices. The example subtopic leads to the article template; replace it with your own page links when reusing the menu.

Use Tab to move between links and controls, and Enter or Space to open or close a focused summary. Subtopic links use tabindex="0" so Tab also reaches them in Safari with its default keyboard settings. Keyboard focus has a visible outline. On phones the panel spans the bar's available width; long menus scroll vertically inside the viewport.

The article template also demonstrates rel="prev" and rel="next" navigation links. They align to the right of the top bar; update their destinations for your articles. Use aria-current="page" on the link to the current page, as in this demo's top bar.

Glass panels

The top bar, footer, article sheet, grid cards and tables already use translucent glass surfaces. Add liquid-glass to a standalone box to reuse the blur, reflections and shadows.

A reusable glass panel

This is class="box liquid-glass". The box provides spacing and rounded corners; the extra class supplies the glass surface.

<div class="box liquid-glass">
    <p>Your content here</p>
</div>

The stylesheet's --glass-opacity variable controls surface opacity while text stays solid. Browsers without backdrop blur get a more opaque background. In light mode, supported browsers and HDR displays can show a brighter top reflection using color(rec2100-linear 2 2 2); otherwise the standard reflection remains.

Automatic appearance and layout

Dark mode

The website follows your device's light or dark appearance through prefers-color-scheme. Change your device appearance to preview both versions of this page. Backgrounds, text, glass surfaces and topic and status colours adapt together; no extra class is needed.

Responsive grids

Resize the window to try the existing image and card grids. They show three columns above 900px, two from 601px to 900px, and one at 600px or below. An incomplete last row stays centred. Headings and article padding also become smaller on narrower screens.

Reduced motion

Image zoom, the moving image sheen and the card lift on hover run only when prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference matches. Enable reduced motion in your device's accessibility settings to view the page without those effects.

Skip to content

Press Tab after loading the page to reveal its Skip to content link. Activating it jumps past the navigation to the main content. Keep the link's destination matched to the main element's id when copying the article template.
