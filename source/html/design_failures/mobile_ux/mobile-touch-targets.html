Bad Mobile Touch Targets

When buttons and links are too small to tap comfortably.

Introduction

Touch targets are interactive elements such as buttons, links and icons. When they are too small or too close together, users may accidentally tap the wrong option.

1. EverEye

On EverEye, the option to continue without accepting cookies is displayed as a small, light-grey link. Meanwhile, the other options appear as large buttons, making the smaller option harder to select.

Why Are Small Touch Targets a Problem?

  • Accidental taps: users may select the wrong option.
  • Difficult navigation: small buttons are harder to tap.
  • Accessibility: users with motor difficulties may struggle.
  • Frustration: repeated mistakes slow down navigation.

2. Design Guidelines

Accessibility guidelines recommend minimum touch target sizes to make mobile interfaces easier to use.

Apple

44 x 44 pt

Recommended touch target size.

Material Design

48 x 48 dp

Recommended touch target size.

WCAG 2.2

24 x 24 CSS px

Minimum target size, subject to exceptions.

How Can Touch Targets Be Improved?

  • Make buttons and links large enough to tap easily.
  • Leave sufficient space between interactive elements.
  • Keep important actions clearly visible.
  • Make all choices easy to find and select.

Conclusion

Small touch targets can make mobile navigation difficult and frustrating. Appropriate sizes and spacing create a more accessible and user-friendly experience.
