Users Disability Types

Types of disabilities classification

By Shalini Denti 3 min read

Disabilities classification

By prioritizing inclusivity from the beginning, we create digital environments that not only meet diverse needs but also foster innovation and connection of everyone. Let’s group type of disabilities into categories:

Visual disabilities:

For users who are blind or has a restricted vision it’s essential that an audio form or a more larger size can be fundamentals. Playing also with highlighted words can be really useful and an ordered paragraph can be the key of a better comprehension instead of a chaotic unique paragraph text with no labeling and no images. Alternative methods are implemented to solve this problem as zoom for screen.

Auditory disabilities:

Such as deaf or compromised hearing. It is required alternatives for video or anything that includes sounds such as transcription of the video. In this case are also used (for example) flashing-lights as Set Alarm to ensure that the information can reach beyond the limited hearing ability.

Motor disabilities:

Can include such as tremors or arthritis to paralysis and amputations. These conditions can affect how people interact with digital devices. A solution can be voice interaction, but for those who also have voice disabilities it must be implemented other kind of inputs.

Cognitive disabilities:

for example dyslexia or ADHD, compromise user perception and information can’t be absorbed. Of course there are many solutions like a more interactive website with many intuitive figures that provide visual clarity without overstimulating the user.
